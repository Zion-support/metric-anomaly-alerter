## What it does

Metric Anomaly Alerter learns the seasonal rhythm of your KPIs — revenue, signups, conversion, churn — and alerts within minutes when a metric breaks pattern, with the segments driving the change already identified.

## Key features

- **Seasonal ML baselines** — no static thresholds; alerts adapt to your business cycles.
- **Segment drill-down** — the country, channel, or product driving the anomaly, auto-identified.
- **Alert triage** — severity scoring kills alert fatigue; only real breaks page you.
- **Incident timeline** — anomalies correlated with deploys, campaigns, and outages.
- **Slack-native** — investigate and annotate anomalies without leaving Slack.

## Who it's for

Growth, ops, and finance teams who need to know about KPI breaks in minutes, not at Monday's review.
